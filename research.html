Research

My research centers on large foundation models and their industrial deployment, aiming to advance AI capabilities from the perspectives of data, algorithms, and systems. The following four directions are closely connected and jointly drive next-generation multimodal intelligence in intelligent agents, recommendation, and content creation.

LLM & VLM Data Governance & Synthesis

High-quality data is the cornerstone of LLMs and vision-language models (VLMs). We build large-scale multimodal corpus engineering pipelines that collect, clean, deduplicate, and evaluate heterogeneous data (text, image, audio, and video) for pre-training and fine-tuning.

We also develop data-centric methods such as informative token selection, synthetic data generation, and automated quality inspection, which have supported the training of large foundation models at Alibaba with consistently high data-quality standards.

Projects & Applications

  • Billion-Scale Multimodal Corpus Engineering

    End-to-end data pipeline for Alibaba's foundation models, covering acquisition, cleaning, multilingual alignment, instruction-following data generation, and automated quality control. Enabled efficient convergence of large-scale models.

  • Data Governance Framework for Foundation Models

    Patented framework for multimodal data governance, supporting deduplication, toxicity filtering, privacy desensitization, and informative-token selection to reduce training cost while improving model quality.

LLM & Multimodal Pre-training

We design pre-training paradigms and efficient training systems for large language models (LLMs) and multimodal large language models (MLLMs), with a focus on contrastive language-image learning, cross-modal alignment, and scalable distributed training.

Our work has been deployed in Alibaba's AI search and intelligent assistant, serving a large user base while substantially reducing compute cost, and has powered the AIGC platform with a large volume of generated assets.

Projects & Applications

  • Unified Multimodal Pre-training Framework

    Integrated vision, language, and speech modalities with large-scale global optimization and generative joint training, achieving multi-scale cross-modal semantic alignment and significant generalization gains.

  • Training and Inference Acceleration

    Designed training and inference acceleration algorithms that substantially reduce computational cost while maintaining comparable model performance, including distributed parallelism, mixed-precision strategies, and efficient token selection.

Video Generation

We develop diffusion-based generative models for high-quality and controllable video synthesis, including virtual try-on, human image animation, and camera-controllable background generation.

These techniques have been integrated into Alibaba's AIGC platform, generating a large volume of assets and creating substantial business value in e-commerce content creation.

Agent Systems

We build intelligent agent systems for e-commerce, including product content understanding, buyer/seller agents, and intelligent customer service.

These agents combine multimodal perception, sequential decision making, and reinforcement learning to support tasks such as automated product understanding, personalized recommendation, seller assistance, and customer service dialogue, all deployed at scale within Alibaba's ecosystem.
