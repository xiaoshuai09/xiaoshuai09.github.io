About Me

I am Shuai Xiao, Associate Professor in the School of Artificial Intelligence at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I lead the Gezhi Intelligence Lab (GIL). Prior to that, I was an Algorithm Director at the Algorithm Technology Department of Alibaba Group, where I led a team of researchers and engineers working on multimodal foundation models, large-scale corpus engineering, and pre-training algorithms. Previously, I joined the Artificial Intelligence Department of Ant Group (formerly Ant Financial) through the Alibaba "Ali Star" talent program.

I received my Ph.D. from the School of Electronic Information and Electrical Engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2018, advised by Prof. Hongyuan Zha. From 2016 to 2018, I was a visiting scholar at Georgia Institute of Technology, working with Prof. Le Song. I received my B.Sc. from the Advanced Class in Electronic and Information Engineering at Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 2013.

My research spans statistical machine learning, deep learning, and their applications, with a current focus on multimodal large language models, data governance and synthesis, video generation, and agent systems for search and recommendation.

Gezhi Intelligence Lab (GIL)

格致智能实验室

Inspired by the classical idea of investigating things to extend knowledge, we study intelligent systems through models, data, and empirical validation. Our current work spans foundation models, data-centric AI, and evaluation in real and simulated environments, while remaining open to emerging questions across artificial intelligence.

Research Interests

My research interests lie in large foundation models and their industrial applications, spanning the following four directions:

LLM & VLM Data Governance & Synthesis

Large-scale multimodal corpus engineering, data cleaning, quality evaluation, deduplication, and synthetic data generation for LLM and VLM pre-training.

Learn more →

LLM & Multimodal Pre-training

Pre-training and alignment of LLMs and MLLMs, contrastive language-image learning, efficient training, and scalable distributed systems.

Learn more →

Video Generation

High-quality controllable video generation, virtual try-on, human image animation, and diffusion-based generative models.

Learn more →

Agent Systems

Product content understanding, buyer/seller agents, intelligent customer service, and sequential decision making for e-commerce.

Join the Lab 加入我们

We welcome applications for research assistants and research interns interested in foundation models, data-centric AI, empirical evaluation, and related areas. Specific topics will be discussed based on each applicant’s background and our current research projects.

我们欢迎对基础模型、以数据为中心的人工智能、实证评估及相关方向感兴趣的同学申请科研助理或科研实习生。具体研究课题将结合申请者背景与课题组当前项目共同讨论。

Research Assistants 科研助理

Work closely with the group on ongoing research, including literature review, dataset development, experimentation and analysis, and research prototyping. This role is best suited to applicants who can contribute consistently over a sustained period.

深度参与课题组在研项目，工作可能包括文献调研、数据集构建、实验设计与分析，以及研究原型开发。更适合能够在一段时间内持续、稳定投入的申请者。

Research Interns 科研实习生

Join a focused research project to gain hands-on experience with research methods and experimentation. The scope and duration will be determined according to project needs and the applicant’s availability.

围绕具体课题参与研究实践，积累科研方法与实验经验。工作内容与周期将根据项目需要及申请者可投入时间确定。

What We Look For 基本要求

  • Strong interest in research and willingness to learn independently. 对科研有浓厚兴趣，具备主动学习能力。
  • Solid foundations in machine learning, data science, computer science, or a related field. 具备机器学习、数据科学、计算机或相关领域的基础。
  • Responsible, collaborative, and able to communicate research progress clearly. 认真负责，善于协作，能够清晰沟通研究进展。
  • Prior research or engineering experience is helpful but not required. 有科研或工程实践经验者优先，但不是必需条件。
